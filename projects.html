Project

CubeSat Passive Thermal Control System

Senior design project team

Part of a four-person team designing a passive thermal control system for a 3U CubeSat, using multi-layer insulation and selective surface coatings to keep the battery pack within its operating range across a low-Earth-orbit thermal cycle.

  • Built a lumped-parameter thermal model in Python to iterate on coating selection quickly
  • Kept predicted battery temperature within ±4°C of target across worst-case hot and cold cases
  • Currently building a vacuum chamber test article to validate the model
🎯 Predicted battery temp held within ±4°C of target across the full thermal cycle
